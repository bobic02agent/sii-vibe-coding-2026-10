# Demonstration Return Procedure

Document ID: DEMO-RETURNS. Version: 1.0. Effective for synthetic demonstrations from 2026-10-18.

This fictional voluntary return program is solely for synthetic MVP demonstrations. It is not a statement of legal withdrawal rights or a substitute for actual company rules. Its limits do not determine statutory eligibility.

## Rules

| Rule | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| R-01 | Handle one electronics item with category, model, purchase date, and one usable image. A return reason is optional. |
| R-02 | The voluntary demonstration window is 30 calendar days after purchase, inclusive: elapsed days 0 through 30 qualify; 31 or more do not. Use the current Europe/Warsaw date at each recommendation and disclose the date when applying this rule. |
| R-03 | For acceptance, the employee must explicitly confirm all three facts: the item functions correctly, it includes the original accessories and packaging, and it has not been used beyond inspection needed to identify its condition. No visible signs of use or damage may remain unresolved. |
| R-04 | An image supports observations of visible condition only. It cannot confirm hidden surfaces, functionality, accessory completeness, or previous use. Missing confirmations require additional information, not presumed compliance. |
| R-05 | Explicit employee confirmation of a nonfunctional item, missing original accessories or packaging, or use beyond inspection is grounds for refusal of this voluntary demonstration return. A reported defect can instead be assessed in a new complaint case. |
| R-06 | Suspected visible wear or damage without confirmation, material conflict between statements and image evidence, or uncertainty about resale condition requires specialist assessment. An unusable image instead requires replacement. |
| R-07 | Recommend acceptance only when R-01, R-02, and R-03 are satisfied and no material uncertainty under R-06 remains. Describe acceptance as subject to the employee's final inspection, not a guarantee that the item can be resold. |
| R-08 | Suspected safety hazards require specialist assessment before any acceptance or refusal based on other rules. Do not provide repair instructions. |
| R-09 | No recommendation executes a return, refunds money, creates a shipment, or establishes legal rights. The employee makes the final decision. |

## Application order

1. Check safety concerns and material contradictions under R-06/R-08.
2. Apply any established refusal ground under R-02/R-05; unrelated missing confirmations do not prevent a clear refusal.
3. Request missing intake facts, usable image, or the three employee confirmations.
4. Refer unresolved visible-condition concerns for specialist assessment under R-06.
5. Otherwise recommend acceptance under R-07.

## Synthetic examples

- Ten days since purchase, no visible damage, but no functionality or completeness confirmation: request those facts and usage confirmation under R-03/R-04.
- Ten days since purchase, all three confirmations provided, usable image with no visible concerns: recommend acceptance under R-07, subject to employee inspection.
- Thirty-one days since purchase, with no safety issue or material conflict: recommend refusal of this demonstration program under R-02; do not claim that a legal right has expired.
- The employee reports no wear but the photograph appears to show a substantial scratch: request specialist assessment under R-06 rather than assert that the customer is dishonest.
